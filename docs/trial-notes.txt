Synthetic equipment notes for testing Astral. These are fictional, not product recommendations.
A field camp has 12 people. Each person requires 2 liters of filtered water per day. Its daily filtered-water requirement is therefore 24 liters.
The Aurora filter supplies at most 18 liters per day and weighs 600 grams. It costs 40 fictional credits. It is insufficient as the camp's only filter.
The Boreal filter supplies at most 30 liters per day and weighs 900 grams. It costs 65 fictional credits. It meets the camp's daily requirement with a 6-liter margin, assuming operation at rated capacity.
Both filters require cleaning after every operating day. Neither filter is documented to remove dissolved chemicals. Nothing in these notes establishes medical or real-world safety.
